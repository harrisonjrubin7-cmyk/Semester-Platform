Rules
  • Base only on dark grounds; shade only as text or icons on light. Never swap them.
  • Accents identify things (courses, categories); they never mean status. Status uses the source semantics with glyph + word.
  • At most one accent per object, plus Sterling. Industry is not the action colour; that’s Semester blue.
  • Pewter is reserved for personal and private content.
  • Tokens: --accent-<name> (base) and --accent-<name>-shade.

Don’t
  • Use an accent to mean status. Oxblood is not “error” and Moss is not “success”; status comes from the source & status tokens with a glyph and word.
  • Set body text in a base accent on Parchment, or a shade accent on Ink. Use the pairs in the table.
  • Fill a primary button with an accent. Actions are Semester blue only.
  • Take colours from user content (a club’s brand colour). Map it to the nearest accent.
