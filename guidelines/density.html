Rules
  • Density is a reader setting (Settings › Display); a screen never forces Tight.
  • Set it on a container with style="--density:.86"; components read --sp-* and follow.
  • Operators on wide screens often choose Snug; mobile defaults to Comfortable.
  • If something breaks at Tight, it was relying on padding for its hit area. Fix the target, not the density.
