Rules
  • Motion explains a change of place or state; it’s never decoration.
  • No bounces, overshoot, parallax, scale-on-press or looping animation.
  • Nothing important happens only in motion; the end state must be clear when durations are 0.
  • Never animate layout properties on long lists; fade or slide the container instead.
