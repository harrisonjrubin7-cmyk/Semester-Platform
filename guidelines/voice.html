Principles

  1. Say what it does. Verbs and nouns over adjectives.
  2. Name the source. Official, yours, AI-assisted, connected.
  3. Never claim a status we don’t hold. Planned, pilot and beta are fine.
  4. Errors say what happened, why, and who can fix it.
  5. Buttons name the result. “Drop CHEM 2220”, not “Submit”.
  6. Plain words, short sentences. Grade 8 reading level for student copy.
  7. No emoji, no exclamation marks in product UI.
