Rules
  • Always glyph + word + tone. Colour alone fails for colour-blind readers and on greyscale prints.
  • Words name the source plainly (“Synced from Brightspace”), never the colour.
  • AI-generated content keeps its chip until a person accepts or edits it; then it becomes Personal.
  • Risk red is for blocking states only, never decoration or emphasis.
