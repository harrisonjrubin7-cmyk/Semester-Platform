How it works
  • The reader picks a ground in Settings → Display; it changes only that person’s view and syncs across their devices.
  • Ink and Parchment are the defaults. The other 11 have tokens (--ground-<name>-bg/-panel/-fg) and apply with data-ground="<name>", which remaps --surface-canvas, --surface-base and --text-primary. Each must pass AA in the table below before it’s offered.
  • Accents, Semester blue and source semantics keep their meaning on every ground; components never hard-code a ground colour.
  • “Square” grounds (Industry) pair with the square corner setting; OLED (Oxide) uses true black for battery.
  • Calm mode (data-calm="still") can apply on any ground.

Adding or offering a ground
  1. Define bg, panel and fg tokens in tokens/colors.css and a [data-ground] block.
  2. Body text must pass AA (4.5:1) on both bg and panel in the table above; secondary text at 68% must pass on panel.
  3. Status chips need 3:1 for their line and 4.5:1 for their word on the panel.
  4. Check a real screen with forms, tables and a chart, not only a swatch.
  5. Then add it to the reader setting. The choice is remembered per device, never set per institution.
