Rules
  • Each step up is lighter; never put a darker surface on top of a lighter one.
  • Pair a raised step with a stronger hairline (14%) so edges stay visible for low-vision readers.
  • Use --lift-* only for overlays (menus, sheets); cards stay flat.
  • Reader grounds keep this rule: raised surfaces derive from their panel, one step lighter.
