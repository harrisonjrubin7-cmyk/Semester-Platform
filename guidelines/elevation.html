Rules
  • Never stack shadows: a card inside a dialog has a border, not a second shadow.
  • On Ink, shadows don’t read; use tonal elevation (see that card) and --lift-*.
  • Shadows never mean “clickable”. Interactivity is shown by hover state and cursor.
