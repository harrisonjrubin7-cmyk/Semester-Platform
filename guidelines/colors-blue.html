Rules
  • Blue means “you can act” or “this is trustworthy navigation”. Never use it for decoration or categories.
  • White text on 700 for primary buttons; Ink text on 400 for dark mode.
  • Focus ring: 2px, offset 2px, 600 on light and 400 on dark; never removed.

Don’t
  • Use blue for official-source badges. Official is navy ink with a ◆ glyph, a separate token family.
  • Use 400 or 500 for text on Parchment; both fail AA (see the last row above).
  • Remove focus rings, or signal focus with a colour change alone.
  • Place two primary blue buttons side by side; demote one to secondary.
