Read-your-own-writes

After you submit, the screen shows your change from the command’s receipt (marked “Saving…”) until the read model reaches that version. It never shows the older projection as if your change failed.

Realtime vs refetch

Realtime only for approvals, incidents, P0/P1 support, rollout state and cache invalidation. Everything else refetches on focus or signal; no polling loops.

Guarantees

Outbox in the same transactionNo write without its event; no event without its write

RebuildableAny read model can be rebuilt from events; UI shows Rebuilding
Freshness is dataprojected_at travels with every read; screens never guess
