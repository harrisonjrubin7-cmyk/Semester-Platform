Brand · Positioning

Semester is building the education operating system

The Apple, Microsoft and Google comparison explains how that system combines three strengths. It’s an analogy for the strategy. It doesn’t claim we match those companies’ scale or maturity, and it doesn’t suggest they have nothing in education. They all do.

How it should feel

Experience · like Apple

Planning, courses, study, support and career feel like one product. Your context comes with you between screens and devices.

Promise: “I don’t need to understand the complexity to use it confidently.”

How it should work

Operations · like Microsoft

Faculty, advisors, registrars and staff do real, governed work in role workspaces built on shared identity, permissions, approvals and audit.

Promise: “The right people get a governed way to finish the work, not just a view of it.”

How it should help

Intelligence · like Google

People find the information they’re allowed to see, understand its source and freshness, and turn it into a next step or a handoff to a person.

Promise: “I can find what matters, see why, and know what I can do next.”

One shared foundation · account, memberships, permissions, policy, education graph, workflows, source authority, consent, audit, search, AI gateway. One account doesn’t mean one unrestricted dataset: personal work, official records, sensitive support details and company operations stay separate.
