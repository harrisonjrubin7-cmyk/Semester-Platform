Rules
  • Sterling is the chrome: wordmark, rail icons, neutral data on Ink.
  • Brass is editorial and ceremonial: kickers, dividers, awards. Never for buttons, links or status.
  • Chrome metal gradient only on display-size text (24px+) on Ink; never on body text or fills.
  • Use shades, not bases, for text on light.
