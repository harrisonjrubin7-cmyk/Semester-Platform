Rules
  • Components read semantic tokens only, so [data-theme="dark"] on any ancestor is enough; never hard-code a dark variant.
  • Shadows don’t read on Ink; raise with a lighter surface step (panel → hero → raise) instead.
  • Images keep their own colours; put them on --surface-sunken so edges stay visible.
  • Check both themes before shipping a component card.
