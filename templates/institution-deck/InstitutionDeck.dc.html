What students face
The information exists. It’s just in twelve places.
Holds are found at registration. Deadlines change in a PDF. Students can’t tell which source is official, so they ask your staff.
What Semester is
Is
One student workspace and staff workspaces, reading from your SIS and LMS, with every fact labelled by source.
Isn’t
A replacement for your SIS or LMS, a risk-scoring system, or a way to send student records to AI providers.
Student experience

  • “Not yet confirmed” when your system hasn’t confirmed

Security
Isolation enforced in the database.
  • Row-level security forced on every table; cross-school access tested on every release
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Every capability off by default, with a kill switch
  • Release gates: no evidence, no release
  • HECVAT in the version your institution requires
Privacy and FERPA
School-official terms, under your control.
  • Data processing agreement with FERPA school-official terms
  • Used only for the purposes you authorize; no redisclosure
  • Students control family, advisor and employer sharing — with end dates
  • Subprocessors listed, with 30-day notice of changes

Accessibility
Tested as whole journeys.

  • Screen reader, keyboard and zoom testing on critical journeys
  • Accessibility conformance report before launch
