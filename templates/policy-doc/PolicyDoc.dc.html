Access Control Policy

OwnerFounder (acting Security Lead)Version1.2
Approved byNot yet approvedNext reviewApr 2027
Applies toAll Semester staff, contractors and service accounts

1. Purpose

Make sure people and systems get only the access their job needs, for only as long as they need it, and that every grant can be traced to a person who approved it.

2. Scope

Production and staging on Vercel and Supabase, GitHub, the company Google Workspace, the support desk and any tool that stores customer or institution data. Institution staff access inside the product is governed by each institution's own roles; this policy covers Semester's own people.

3. Rules

  1. Least privilege. Access is granted per role, never per request for convenience.
  2. No standing access to student records. Company staff cannot read student records. Support uses institution-approved, time-boxed break-glass access (4 hours maximum) with a recorded reason.
  3. Strong sign-in. SSO with phishing-resistant MFA on every system that supports it.
  4. Two people for high-impact changes. Production database roles, RLS policy changes and secret rotation need a second approver.
  5. Quarterly review. The owner reviews every grant each quarter and removes anything unused for 30 days.
  6. Leavers same day. Access is removed within 4 working hours of a person leaving.

4. Roles

RoleResponsibility
Policy ownerApproves grants, runs quarterly review, keeps this document current.
ManagerRequests access for their reports and confirms it at review.
Every personUses only their own account, reports suspected misuse within 24 hours.

5. Exceptions

Written, time-limited (90 days maximum), approved by the policy owner and logged in the exception register with a compensating control.

6. Enforcement and evidence

Violations may lead to access removal and disciplinary action. Evidence: access review exports, the audit log, and the exception register. Related: Incident Response Plan, Vendor Risk Policy, Data Retention Policy.
