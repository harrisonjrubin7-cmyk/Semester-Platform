Release 2026.10.2 · Thu 15 Oct 2026

Registration readiness, sooner

Students see holds that block registration three weeks before their window opens, with the office that can clear each one.

For students

✓ New
Readiness checklist on Today. Holds, advising and prerequisites in one list, each with the office that decides.
↻ Improved
Clearer freshness labels. Every synced date now says when it was last checked.

For staff

✓ New
Holds by office (Registrar console). See how many students each office is blocking and preview what they see.
◌ Pilot only
Outreach over 200 students needs a second approver. Opt-outs are honoured automatically.

Fixed

  • Screen readers announced the due date twice on assignment rows.
  • Dark mode chips on the Grades page dropped below 4.5:1 contrast.
  • Time zones in calendar exports for students abroad.

Known issues

  • Housing holds from StarRez can be up to 6 hours behind. The label says so.
What stays the same

YES is still where you register and the registrar's record is still the official one. Nothing in this release changes what data the AI can see.
