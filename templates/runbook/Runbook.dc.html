Runbook R4 · Operate

SIS sync stalled

Trigger
No successful sync for 2 h
Default severity
! SEV-2 · {{ sevNote }}
Owner
On-call engineer
Target
Fresh data in 4 h

First 15 minutes

  1. 1
    Acknowledge the alert and open an incident in the ops inbox. Name yourself incident lead.
  2. 2
    Confirm the freshness banner is showing to students: “Your registration status hasn't updated since 09:10. Check YES before you act on it.”
  3. 3
    Pause outbound reminders that depend on hold or registration data.
  4. 4
    Post in #incidents: what is stale, since when, who is affected, next update time.

Diagnose

CheckWhereIf yes
Auth to SIS failing (401/403)?ops.sync_runs.errorAsk institution IT to rotate the service credential.
SIS returning 5xx or timing out?connector logsTheir outage: back off, notify the institution contact.
Job queue backed up?queue depth panelScale workers; check for a poison record.
Schema mismatch after an SIS upgrade?mapping validatorFreeze imports; fix mapping in staging first.

Fix and verify

  • Re-run sync for one cohort first.
  • Compare 20 sampled records with the SIS.
  • Clear the banner only when the read model is under 15 min old.
  • Resume reminders.

Rollback

  • Revert the connector to the last good release tag.
  • Never hand-edit synced records: the SIS is the system of record.
  • Replay from the last good cursor.

Escalate

Over 4 h, or wrong data shown to a student: raise to SEV-1, page the founder, and tell the institution contact within 1 h. After close, write a blameless review within 5 working days.
