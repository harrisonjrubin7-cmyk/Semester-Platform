Fewer students locked out on registration day

A one-term pilot with first-year students showed holds earlier and routed each one to the office that could clear it.

−38%
students with an unresolved hold at window open
2.4 days
median time to clear a hold, down from 6.1
71%
weekly active students in the pilot cohort

Method: pilot cohort vs the prior fall, same class year. Source: institution SIS export, analysed by the registrar's office.

The challenge

Holds sat in four offices' systems. Students found out on the morning their window opened, and front-desk queues tripled that week.

What was deployed

  • Student Today with a readiness checklist, read from the SIS every 15 minutes.
  • Registrar console: readiness by cohort and holds by office.
  • SAML SSO; read-only connectors. No data written back to the SIS.
“The students who used to show up at the counter on day one had already cleared their holds.”
