Problem
A semester is scattered across a dozen systems. Students assemble it by hand.
Deadlines live in the LMS and in PDFs. Holds live in the SIS. Changes arrive by email. Nothing says which fact is official, or what to do next.
What it costs
Students
Missed deadlines, late holds, registration surprises — discovered too late to fix.
Staff
Advisors and registrars answer the same questions because no one shares one record.
Institutions
More tools, more integrations, and AI arriving without governance.

Trust, built in
Institutions keep their records. Students keep their work.
Isolation
Row-level security on every table; one school can never read another’s data.
Authority
The SIS stays the system of record. Semester shows a labelled copy.
Control
Every capability is off by default and switched on per school, with an approver.
Governed AI
AI that helps students study — and never sees their records.
  • One gateway for every AI request, with a kill switch
  • Drafts only from course materials faculty approved
  • Each course sets its own AI policy
  • AI output is labelled, and never becomes an official record
Why now
Standards make connecting cheaper: LTI 1.3, OneRoster 1.2, SSO.
Students already use AI. Schools need a governed version.
Public institutions face WCAG 2.1 AA deadlines in 2027–28.
Traction
Signed paid pilot with Vanderbilt — launch Spring 2027, pending institutional activation.
Student records are used only after activation, under the agreement’s FERPA school-official terms.
