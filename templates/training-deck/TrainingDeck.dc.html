Using the registrar console

45 minutes · bring a laptop signed in with SSO

By the end you can

  1. Find students who can't register yet, and why
  2. Preview exactly what a student sees
  3. Approve an override with a recorded reason
  4. Tell when data is stale

Read the labels first

◆
Official
From your SIS. The record.
⇄
Connected
Synced copy. Check the time.
○
Student entered
The student's own note or plan.
✦
AI-assisted
A suggestion. A person decides.

Step 1 of 3

Open Readiness and pick a cohort.

The bar shows how many students are clear, blocked, or waiting on an office.

readiness screen

Step 2 of 3

Choose “Student view” before you call.

You see what they see. Opening it is logged.

student view preview

Step 3 of 3

Approve an override with a reason.

The reason is required and goes into the decision trail. The SIS is updated by your team, not by Semester.

override dialog

✓ Do

  • Check the “last synced” time
  • Write reasons a colleague would understand
  • Send outreach from the console so opt-outs apply

⊘ Don't

  • Screenshot student records into email
  • Treat an AI suggestion as a decision
  • Share your session

Check yourself

A hold shows ⇄ synced 7 hours ago. The student says it was cleared this morning. What do you do?

Answer: check the SIS directly, then tell the student it may take until the next sync to update.
