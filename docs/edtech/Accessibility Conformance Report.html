Accessibility Conformance Report

VPAT 2.5 (WCAG 2.2 edition) summary for Semester web and mobile web · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold. This summary must be completed against real audit results before it is given to a buyer.

1. Product and evaluation

Product: Semester web application and mobile web, including Student, Faculty, Advisor, Institution, Studio and Social workspaces. Target: WCAG 2.2 Level AA, Section 508 and EN 301 549. Evaluation method: automated scanning in CI, manual keyboard and screen-reader testing (NVDA + Firefox, VoiceOver + Safari), and an independent audit before first institutional contract.
