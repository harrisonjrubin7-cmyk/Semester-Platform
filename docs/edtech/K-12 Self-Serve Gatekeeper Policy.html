K-12 Self-Serve Gatekeeper Policy

How self-serve signup stays safe when a teacher uses a K-12 email · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. The risk

A teacher who signs up self-serve and uploads a roster of under-13 students could cause a COPPA violation if neither the school nor parents have consented. Self-serve terms cannot replace a district agreement.

2. Gatekeeper flow

  1. Detect K-12 context at signup: domain list (k12, district domains), role answer “K-12 teacher”, or roster upload with birthdates under 13
  2. Pause before any student data is accepted
  3. Ask: “Is your school or district already a Semester customer?” → if yes, join via SSO and inherit the district agreement
  4. If no, offer two paths: (a) teacher-only workspace with no student accounts or student data, or (b) “Request a district agreement” which notifies sales and the privacy lead
  5. Never accept student personal information for under-13 users without a signed Student Data Privacy Addendum or NDPA naming the authorizing official

3. Product rules

  • Roster import disabled for K-12 self-serve accounts
  • Social, messaging between students and public profiles off for under-13
  • Higher-ed professors and adult students may accept the standard, student-safe Terms of Service

4. Already built

The mock signup in the prototype includes this gatekeeper; production implementation is in build stream 02 (identity and onboarding).
