NDPA Readiness and Crosswalk

Mapping Semester’s Student Data Privacy Addendum to the National Data Privacy Agreement · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Purpose

Many U.S. districts require the Student Data Privacy Consortium’s National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) with state-specific Exhibit G. Semester will sign the NDPA as the standard K-12 instrument and keep its own Student Data Privacy Addendum for higher education.
