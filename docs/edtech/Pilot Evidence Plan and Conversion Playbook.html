Pilot Evidence Plan and Conversion Playbook

What a pilot must prove before it becomes a paid contract · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Success criteria (agreed at kickoff)

MetricTargetMeasured by
Students activated (2 meaningful actions in week 1)≥ 60% of invitedProduct events
Weekly active students≥ 40% by week 6Product events
Advisors using caseload weekly≥ 70% of pilot advisorsUsage log
Registration-readiness items cleared before window+15 pts vs. last termRegistrar report
Support tickets per 100 students≤ 5Support desk

2. Timeline

  • Week 0: kickoff, criteria signed, data agreement live
  • Weeks 1–6: weekly sponsor update
  • Week 8: readout with evidence
  • Week 10: conversion decision

3. Conversion triggers

Meeting 3 of 5 criteria triggers the paid-contract proposal at the pilot price. Missing criteria get a 30-day remediation plan, not a discount.

4. Evidence rules

Aggregate only; groups under 10 suppressed; no student-level data in sales materials.
