FERPA Compliance Statement

How Semester acts as a school official for education records · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

This statement does not by itself satisfy FERPA. Whether the school-official pathway applies depends on the actual service relationship, the institution’s direct control, legitimate educational interest, authorized use and redisclosure limits, which are set in each institution’s agreement and annual notice.

1. Role under FERPA

When an institution uses Semester, Semester acts as a school official with a legitimate educational interest under 34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B): it performs a service the institution would otherwise use its own staff for, under the institution’s direct control over use and maintenance of education records, and uses records only for the authorized purpose.

2. Commitments

  • Education records stay the institution’s. Semester gains no ownership and no right to use them for its own purposes, including advertising, sale or training general AI models
  • Access is limited by role and by relationship (teaching assignment, advising caseload, case assignment) and checked on every request; a title alone never grants access
  • No redisclosure except to approved subprocessors listed in the Subprocessor Register, bound to equal protections
  • Student private work (notes, drafts, Brain conversations) is kept separate from official records and not visible to staff
  • Every access to a record is logged with role, scope, purpose and time; the institution can export the log

4. Disclosure records

Semester keeps operational access logs separately from the institution’s §99.32 disclosure record. Disclosures Semester makes on the institution’s behalf (e.g. official transcripts) are written to the disclosure ledger the institution controls.

5. Evidence

Permission checker and negative-access tests (trust/permissions), DC-02 revocation lab, FERPA checklist (trust/ferpa), direct-control drills (trust/drills), Data Protection Addendum, Student Data Privacy Addendum.
