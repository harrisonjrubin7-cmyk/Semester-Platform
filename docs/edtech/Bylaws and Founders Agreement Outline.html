Bylaws and Founders Agreement Outline

What the corporate records must cover for a Delaware C-Corp · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Bylaws (outline)

  • Stockholder meetings, notice, quorum, written consent
  • Board: size (start at 1–3), election, removal, vacancies, written consent
  • Officers: CEO, Secretary, Treasurer; duties and removal
  • Stock certificates or uncertificated shares; transfer restrictions; right of first refusal
  • Indemnification and advancement for directors and officers
  • Fiscal year; amendments

3. Board records to keep

  • Initial board consent: officers, bylaws, stock issuance, bank, fiscal year
  • Equity plan adoption and each grant
  • 409A valuation before option grants
  • Approval of material contracts and financing
