COPPA Compliance Policy

How Semester handles users under 13 when K-12 schools use it · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Scope

Applies when a K-12 school or district enables Semester for students under 13, and to any self-serve teacher account that would add such students. Semester’s launch market is higher education; K-12 use is off by default until a district agreement is signed.

2. Consent model

Semester relies on school authorization in place of parental consent only where the school uses Semester solely for the school’s educational purpose and for no commercial purpose. Otherwise verifiable parental consent is required before any child’s personal information is collected.

  • District or school signs the Student Data Privacy Addendum (or NDPA) naming the authorizing official
  • The authorizing official attests the use is educational only
  • Parents receive the school’s direct notice with Semester’s data practices and can review and request deletion through the school

3. Self-serve gatekeeper

If someone signs up with a K-12 domain, or says they teach students under 13, Semester blocks roster upload and student invitations until either (a) they confirm authority to consent for the school and the school has an agreement on file, or (b) they request a district agreement. Teacher-only use without student data stays available.

5. Use, retention and deletion

  • No advertising, profiling or sale; no use to train general-purpose AI
  • Retained only while the school’s agreement is active and needed for the educational purpose
  • Deleted within 60 days of the school’s instruction or contract end; backups expire within 90 days
  • Parents’ review and deletion requests are honored through the school within 10 business days

6. Security and accountability

Same controls as the Security Policies, plus age-aware feature flags enforced server-side. Named owner: Privacy lead (founder until hired). Reviewed every year and before any K-12 launch.
