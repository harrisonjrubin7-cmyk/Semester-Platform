AI Boundary and Explainability Charter

What Semester’s AI will and won’t do, in writing · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Promise

Semester’s AI helps people explain, summarize, study, plan, research and navigate. It never independently issues grades, changes academic records, enrolls or drops students, approves financial actions, or makes discipline, admission, aid or eligibility decisions. Those stay with authorized people and official systems.

2. How it’s enforced

BoundaryMechanism
No official actionsAI has no write path to records, registration or finance; only the transaction service can act, and only on a person’s confirmed request
Rules decide, AI explainsEligibility and requirement results come from versioned rule engines; AI text is checked against the structured result and replaced by template text if it disagrees
Smallest contextOnly context the user selected or the course allows; private notes, other courses and DMs off by default
SourcesEvery recommendation names the artifact, rule version or record it relies on
Course policyAssignment-level AI-use rules applied before any response
No training on customer dataInstitutional and student data are never used to train general models; zero-retention settings with model providers
Human pathEvery AI surface offers a person to contact
AuditPrompts, context IDs, model version and outputs logged for review; students can export or delete their history

3. Never inferred

Mental health, finances, disability, academic risk or personal relationships. No opaque “risk scores” shown to students.

4. Governance

Model release gate (7 items), AI RMF controls, incident kill switches. See AI Governance Policy.
