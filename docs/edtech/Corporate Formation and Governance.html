2. Operating Agreement (outline)

  • Name, purpose, principal office, registered agent
  • Member: the founder, 100% interest
  • Management by the member; officers may be appointed
  • Capital contributions and distributions
  • Books, records and bank accounts separate from personal finances
  • Indemnification and limited liability
  • Transfer restrictions and admission of new members
  • Dissolution
  • Conversion clause for a future C-Corp

3. Before a priced round

  • Convert to a Delaware C-Corp
  • Adopt bylaws, issue founder stock with 4-year vesting and 1-year cliff, file 83(b) within 30 days
  • Assign IP from the LLC to the corporation
  • Board minutes for each issuance
