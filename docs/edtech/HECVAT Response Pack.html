Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. How to use

The Higher Education Community Vendor Assessment Toolkit (EDUCAUSE) is the standard university security questionnaire. This pack holds Semester’s current answers by section so sales can send a completed HECVAT within two business days. Answer honestly: “Planned” is better than an unsupported “Yes.”
