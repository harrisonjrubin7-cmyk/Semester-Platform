Campus Ambassador Playbook

Recruit, train and support student and instructor advocates · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

Roles

RoleCommitmentSupport
Campus ambassador (student)4 hrs/week, one semester$15/hr stipend, swag, reference letter
Faculty championUse Semester in one courseEarly features, office hours with the team

Rules

  • Ambassadors disclose they are paid by Semester in every post
  • No access to other students’ data; no sign-ups on someone else’s behalf
  • No incentives tied to sign-up counts that encourage spam
  • Follow the Community Guidelines and the institution’s solicitation rules

Monthly cadence

  • Week 1: tabling or class demo (with instructor permission)
  • Week 2: feedback session
  • Week 3: content (one honest post)
  • Week 4: report: what worked, what students asked
