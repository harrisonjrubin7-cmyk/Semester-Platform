Educator Terms

Student-safe terms for teachers and professors who sign up on their own · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Who these terms are for

Individual instructors using Semester without an institution agreement. Students invited by an instructor accept the student version of these terms.

2. Ownership

You and your students own what you create. Semester gets only the limited right to host and process it to run the service.

3. Student data

  • No advertising, behavioral tracking, profiling or sale
  • No model training on student content
  • Students can export and delete their own work
  • Under-13 students can’t be added without a school agreement (see the COPPA policy)

4. When your school signs up

If your institution signs an agreement, its terms replace these for your account and your data moves under its control.

5. Plans and billing

Free for instructors during the pilot period. Paid features, if any, are shown before purchase and renew only with notice.
