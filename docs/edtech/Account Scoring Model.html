Account Scoring Model

When a self-serve campus becomes an enterprise lead · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

Signals

SignalPoints
Each active instructor account at the same institution+5
Each 25 active students at the institution+3
An instructor connects an LMS course+8
Department admin or staff signs up+10
Pricing or security page visits from the domain (aggregate)+4
Request for SSO, HECVAT or DPA+20

Thresholds

ScoreAction
30+Marketing: send institution overview to known sponsors
50+Sales: AE outreach within 5 business days
80+Founder call; propose pilot

Scores use account-level aggregates only; no individual student data is used for sales.
