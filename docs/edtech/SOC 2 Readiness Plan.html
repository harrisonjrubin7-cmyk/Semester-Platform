SOC 2 Readiness Plan

Path to a SOC 2 Type II report · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Target

SOC 2 Type I (design of controls) within 6 months of first paid institutional contract; Type II (operating effectiveness over a 6-month window) within 12–15 months. Trust Services Criteria in scope: Security (required), Availability, Confidentiality. Privacy considered after Type II.
