# SSO, invitation and account linking

_Semester growth and onboarding · 2026-10-05 · Prototype: ui_kits/journey/index.html. Code: handoff/semester-platform (110_onboarding_growth.sql, lib/auth/return-path.ts, tests/onboarding.test.ts). Design-stage until code, tests and release evidence exist._

1. Campus invite, landing page or deep link 2. Institution detected or selected 3. SSO or secure invitation 4. Membership validated 5. Account created or linked 6. Role/capability mapping from the institution 7. Institution notices and consent 8. Official data loaded with source/freshness 9. Role onboarding 10. Correct workspace.

Never force a CIO, registrar or student-success leader through student signup. "I don't see my institution" falls back to self-serve with later linking. If SSO fails, explain why it is required and hand off to campus IT. Linking a personal account to an institution account requires both to be verified; data does not merge without the student's consent.
