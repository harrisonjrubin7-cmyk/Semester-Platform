# Trust Center and procurement evidence standard

**Status:** Priority draft; public claims require legal, security, and executive approval.

## Purpose
Publish only accurate, scoped, dated claims and supply controlled evidence to institutional buyers without exposing sensitive implementation details or customer data.

## Claim taxonomy

- Designed: a documented intended control or capability.
- Implemented: code/configuration exists in a named scope.
- Tested: dated reproducible evidence exists.
- Independently reviewed/certified: only when the named external review or certification is complete and current.
- Institution-authorized/production-enabled: scope-specific activation evidence exists.

## Procurement workflow

Intake question → locate approved answer/control → confirm scope and evidence date → tailor only without broadening claim → reviewer approval → disclose under appropriate confidentiality → archive response/version → schedule refresh.

## Required content controls

- Do not say “compliant,” “certified,” “secure,” or “no data retention” without exact supporting scope and evidence.
- Distinguish company policy, provider terms, tenant configuration, and verified runtime behavior.
- HECVAT responses identify exact questionnaire version, applicability, evidence, gaps, owners, and review date; completion is not certification.
- Accessibility representations require scoped evaluation evidence and do not substitute for an institution’s own review.

## Gate
A public claim is blocked without an approved evidence link, owner, scope, date, reviewer, and expiry/review schedule.
