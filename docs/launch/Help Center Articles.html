Help center: first 10 articles

Student-facing. Plain language, under 120 words each. Publish in the in-app Help and on the support site.

1. Sign in with your school account

Choose your institution on the sign-in page and use your normal school login. Semester never sees your password. If you can’t sign in, check the status page, then contact your IT help desk.

2. What does “Official” mean?

◆ Official facts come straight from your school’s systems, with the time they were last updated. If something looks wrong, the school’s record is correct: use “Flag for registrar”.

3. Understand a registration hold

Open the hold to see what it is, which office owns it, and whether it blocks registration. Use “Contact” to reach that office; Semester shows the status but can’t remove holds.

4. Share your plan with an advisor

Go to Plan → Share. Pick the person, what to share, why, and until when. You can revoke it any time in Privacy and data.

5. Ask Semester and your data

Ask Semester answers from your school’s published sources and cites them. It never uses your grades, holds or aid, and you can turn it off in Privacy and data.

6. Why an AI answer was refused

Your instructor or school may not allow AI for that task, or your question included a student record. The message tells you which and who can help.

7. Export or delete your data

Privacy and data → Export my data for a copy. Request deletion to remove your notes, tasks and files within 30 days; official records follow your school’s rules.

8. Turn on calm mode and reader settings

Settings → Display: choose a ground, text size and calm mode (less motion and fewer notifications).

9. Sign out of other devices

Privacy and data → Signed-in devices → Sign out.

10. Report a problem or unsafe content

Use Report on any post or listing, or Help → Contact support. In an emergency, call 911 or campus safety; Semester is not monitored for emergencies.
