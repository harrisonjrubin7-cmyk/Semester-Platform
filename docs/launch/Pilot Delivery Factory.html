2. Reusable kit

  • Onboarding questionnaire and tenant templates by segment
  • Prebuilt role bundles and policies
  • SSO/SCIM guide, LTI and OneRoster wizards
  • Reconciliation dashboard and cutover plan
  • Training curriculum, staff flier, student quick start, welcome email, poster
  • Weekly sponsor update and incident notice emails
  • Results report template and case-study consent

3. Capacity

One implementation lead can run two concurrent pilots. A third concurrent pilot is the trigger to hire (docs/run/Hiring Plan.html).

4. Metrics

  • Days from signature to launch (target ≤ 45)
  • Gates passed first time
  • Activation in week 1
  • Measures met at term end
  • Pilot-to-annual conversion
