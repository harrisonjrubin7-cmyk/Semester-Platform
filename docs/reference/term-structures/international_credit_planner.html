Credit converter

2 US-to-ECTS is a configurable convention; 2 CATS = 1 ECTS. Australian factor must come from the institution's approved policy; no universal factor is supplied. Workload hours are NOT used to award credit.

Degree planning model

Illustrative totals only. All entries must use the SAME selected credit system. Pending transfer is an unapproved scenario, not completed credit. Term count ignores prerequisites, availability, residency, grade rules and scheduling constraints.
