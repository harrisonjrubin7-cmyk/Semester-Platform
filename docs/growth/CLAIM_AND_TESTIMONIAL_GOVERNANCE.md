# Claim and testimonial governance

_Semester growth and onboarding · 2026-10-05 · Prototype: ui_kits/journey/index.html. Code: handoff/semester-platform (110_onboarding_growth.sql, lib/auth/return-path.ts, tests/onboarding.test.ts). Design-stage until code, tests and release evidence exist._

Follows FTC endorsement guidance (16 CFR Part 255): endorsements reflect honest experience, claims are substantiated, material connections disclosed.

- No fake testimonials, logos, customers, reviews, outcomes, app-store badges, download counts, certifications, integrations or pricing.
- Institution names and results only with written permission (Vanderbilt name cleared for public use; no results yet).
- Every number cites a source or the finance model; forecasts labelled.
- Screenshots are real or labelled "prototype".
- Claim review before publish: founder + (when hired) counsel for legal/regulatory claims.
