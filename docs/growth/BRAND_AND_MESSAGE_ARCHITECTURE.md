# Brand and message architecture

_Semester growth and onboarding · 2026-10-05 · Prototype: ui_kits/journey/index.html. Code: handoff/semester-platform (110_onboarding_growth.sql, lib/auth/return-path.ts, tests/onboarding.test.ts). Design-stage until code, tests and release evidence exist._

Headline: "The Education Operating System that turns student intent into action." Sub: "Semester brings planning, learning, registration readiness, academic support, and institution-approved guidance into one native, governed experience."

## Accurate language

- Built natively for education.
- Designed to become the connected operating layer.
- Deployable beside existing systems during a controlled transition.
- Official data is labelled by source and authority.
- Native-authoritative domains are introduced only after governed cutover.

Never claim Semester has replaced an LMS, SIS, registrar or payment system until true and evidenced.

## Design language

Shared with the app: Ink chrome, Parchment surfaces, Semester Blue for action/focus, Barlow body, Barlow Condensed headings, Cinzel only for rare editorial moments, source/status vocabulary. Marketing adds editorial whitespace, real (or labelled) product visuals, outcome-led stories, prominent trust evidence, simple CTAs.
