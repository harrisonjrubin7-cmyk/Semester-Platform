RBAC and Entitlement Matrix

Who can see and do what in Semester · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Model

Roles grant capabilities; relationships scope them to specific records; policy and legal restrictions can still deny. Access = capability AND in-scope relationship AND policy allows AND no restriction. Default is deny.

3. Rules

  • Teaching never implies advising access, and vice versa
  • Assignments expire; access ends the same day
  • Delegated approvals name scope, unit and expiry
  • Every check also applies to exports, jobs, caches, search and AI requests
