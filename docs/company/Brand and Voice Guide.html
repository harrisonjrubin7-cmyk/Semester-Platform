Brand and Voice Guide

How Semester looks and sounds · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Positioning

Semester is the place where educational life comes together: clear next steps with their official source, built with institutions, never around them.

2. Voice

  • Calm, specific, supportive: “Your registration window opens Monday at 7 am” not “Don’t miss out!”
  • Plain verbs: plan, verify, submit, review
  • Say what’s uncertain: “may need review,” “I couldn’t confirm”
  • Never shame or pressure students; never imply productivity equals worth
  • Say “designed to” for capabilities not yet live

3. Visual

Use the design system tokens, type and logo in styles.css and assets/. Contrast at least 4.5:1; never put text on busy imagery; every image has alt text.

4. Co-branding

Institution logos only with written permission and the institution’s own brand rules.
