IP Assignment Structure

Layered ownership, not an NDA · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Layers

LayerDocumentCovers
Existing founder workFounder IP assignment (present assignment of specifically identified assets)Pre-formation code, designs, docs, domains
Ongoing founder and employee workConfidentiality and inventions assignment agreementCovered work during service
Contractor workServices agreement with express present assignmentDeliverables, background IP, payment, confidentiality
Pre-existing assetsBackground-IP schedule and licenseExcluded assets Semester still needs to use
Ownership evidenceIP chain-of-title registerEach asset → creator → assignment or license

2. Record for each material asset

  • Creator and creation period
  • Location (repository, design file, drive)
  • Ownership basis and the signed assignment or license
  • Prior-employer, university, sponsor or collaborator obligations
  • Third-party components and their restrictions
  • Exclusions and any background-IP license needed

3. Cautions

  • Nobody can assign rights they don’t own
  • “Work made for hire” wording alone doesn’t cover every contractor case
  • Review university and sponsored-work obligations before telling investors Semester owns its IP; this is a review step, not a conclusion that a university owns it
