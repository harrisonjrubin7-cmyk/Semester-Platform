Financing Documents Guide

What paperwork each kind of round needs, and how to track it · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Three separate categories

  • Company financial records (books, statements, forecasts)
  • Investor-facing materials (deck, summary, model, data room)
  • Binding financing documents (SAFEs, notes, priced-round agreements)

2. By instrument

StructureCore documents
SAFEExecuted SAFE per investor (post-money: valuation cap, discount, or uncapped MFN); optional pro rata side letter (not automatic); board approval; financing register; funds receipt; dilution model
Convertible noteNote purchase agreement; promissory notes; side letters; approvals; interest and maturity tracking; conversion model
Priced seedTerm sheet; charter amendment; stock purchase agreement; investor and governance agreements; disclosure schedules; approvals; closing records
Series A (NVCA model set)Amended and restated certificate of incorporation; stock purchase agreement; investors’ rights agreement; voting agreement; ROFR and co-sale agreement

Use the current NVCA forms chosen by transaction counsel, not an old download.

3. Priced-round closing package (as negotiated)

  • Term sheet and disclosure schedules
  • Board and stockholder approvals
  • Pre- and post-financing cap tables; SAFE and note conversion calculations
  • Option pool or plan amendments
  • Director indemnification agreements; investor side letters
  • Closing certificates, good standing, legal opinion if negotiated
  • Funds-flow memo and closing checklist
  • Securities notices (e.g. Form D and state notices)
  • Post-closing obligations calendar

5. Model hygiene

Never present bookings, cash receipts, recognized revenue, ARR and unpaid pilot interest as interchangeable. Model individual subscriptions, institutional subscriptions, implementation fees, support or services, AI and infrastructure cost, and pilot conversion separately.
