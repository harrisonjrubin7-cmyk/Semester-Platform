2. Tiers

TierWhenRequired
1Restricted data or production accessSOC 2 or equivalent, DPA, subprocessor terms, U.S. hosting, breach notice ≤ 72 h, deletion on exit, Trust + founder approval
2Confidential company dataSecurity questionnaire, DPA if personal data, SSO
3Public or no dataFounder approval, SSO where possible

3. After approval

  • Add to vendor inventory and, if Tier 1, the public Subprocessor Register (30-day notice to institutions)
  • Renewal date in the calendar; review yearly
  • Exit plan: how to export and delete
