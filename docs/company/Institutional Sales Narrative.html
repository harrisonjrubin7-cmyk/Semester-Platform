Institutional Sales Narrative

Slide-by-slide outline for provosts, CIOs and student-success leaders · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Deck outline (12 slides)

  1. Title: Semester, the education operating system
  2. The problem: students juggle 10+ portals; staff can’t see the whole student; holds and missed deadlines cost retention
  3. What changes: one place for planning, learning, registration, advising, career and campus life
  4. The Action Center: “what matters next,” with the official source and the office responsible
  5. Semester Brain: AI that explains with sources and never acts on records
  6. For staff: coordinated caseloads, referrals and consoles
  7. Safe adoption: mirror → assist → parallel run → cutover, module by module
  8. Trust: FERPA school-official model, Student Data Privacy Addendum, HECVAT, accessibility
  9. Outcomes we’ll measure together (pilot evidence plan)
  10. Pricing: core + modules, pilot credited to year one
  11. Pilot plan and timeline (12 weeks)
  12. Ask: sponsor, IT contact, decision date

2. Talk track rules

  • Lead with retention, staff time and student experience, not feature count
  • Say “designed to” for capabilities not yet in production
  • No outcome claims without a cited pilot
  • Always offer the Trust Packet
