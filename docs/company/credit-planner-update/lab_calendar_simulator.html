Customize the formula

Academic hours = lecture credits × (lecture contact + lecture independent hours/credit) + lab credits × (lab contact + lab independent hours/credit). Divide each component by teaching weeks; add weekly life commitments separately.

All inputs use 60-minute clock-hour planning units. Defaults are illustrative, not legal minima. Credit parts are disjoint: do not count a combined course's full credits in both lecture and lab fields.

Scope and evidence

Comparisons use identical semester-equivalent credits and activity budgets across 16-week semester, 10-week quarter, 12-week trimester and 4-week block windows. Quarter native units must be converted under approved policy before entry. These are illustrative durations, not university calendars.

No clinical burnout score is produced. Workload and lab-capacity flags are user-defined planning triggers. Actual instruction, credit equivalence, accessibility, laboratory safety, equipment and financial-aid treatment require institutional review. Uniform weekly allocation does not model assessment peaks or cancellations.

SUNY credit/contact-hour policy illustrates distinct lab equivalents and 50-minute instructional periods. Do not substitute these defaults for a university's approved policy.
