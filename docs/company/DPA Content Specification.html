DPA Content Specification

What Semester’s DPA must say, and the facts needed first · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Roles are per data, not blanket

Semester processes institution-provided records on the institution’s behalf, but has its own responsibilities for billing, account administration and security logs. The DPA names which data each role covers.

2. Core provisions (GDPR Art. 28 baseline)

ProvisionRequired substance
Parties and rolesCustomer, Semester legal entity, covered processing
ScopeSubject matter, duration, nature, purposes, data types, data subjects
InstructionsDocumented instructions incl. transfers; handling of unlawful instructions
ConfidentialityDuties on authorized personnel
SecurityAccurate technical and organizational measures (annex)
SubprocessorsAuthorization, change notice, objection, flow-down
Rights assistanceHelp with access, deletion, correction requests
IncidentsNotification and cooperation
AssessmentsHelp with DPIAs and prior consultation where required
Return and deletionCustomer’s choice at end; legal retention; backups
AssuranceCompliance information and audit rights
TransfersMechanism, annexes, assessments
Liability and precedenceRelation to main agreement without overriding mandatory rights

3. California (where CCPA applies, Semester as service provider)

  • Limited, specified purposes
  • Same level of privacy protection
  • No selling or sharing
  • No use outside the business purpose; no impermissible combination of data
  • Customer oversight rights
  • Notice if Semester can’t meet its obligations
  • Customer right to stop and remediate unauthorized use

4. Semester annexes (placeholders until verified)

  • Purposes, data subjects, data categories
  • Verified security measures
  • Subprocessors and locations
  • Retention, deletion, backup rotation, legal holds
  • International transfers
  • Education-record authorization and privacy schedules
  • Student-private workspace boundaries
  • AI providers, permitted inputs, retention, no-training terms: must match real provider settings before signing
