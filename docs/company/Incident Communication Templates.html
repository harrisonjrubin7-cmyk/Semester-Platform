Incident Communication Templates

Messages for outages, security and privacy incidents · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer. Privacy and breach notices must be reviewed by counsel before sending.

1. Status page: outage

“We’re investigating an issue affecting [feature] for [who]. Your work is saved. Next update by [time].” Then: identified → monitoring → resolved, each with time.

2. Institution notice: security incident

“On [date] we identified [what happened]. Affected: [systems, data classes, number of records if known]. We have [contained/what]. Your action: [any]. Our contact: [name, phone]. We’ll update you by [time].” Sent to the named institution contacts within the contract window (72 hours of confirmation for education records, or sooner where law requires).

3. User notice: privacy incident

Only after the institution agrees for institutional accounts. Plain language: what happened, what data, what we did, what you can do, who to contact.

4. Rules

  • Facts only; no speculation or blame
  • Never promise a cause or timeline you can’t keep
  • One owner approves every message; log sends in the incident record
