1. Status scale

Unassessed → Draft → Reviewed → Executed or filed → Verified. A generated form is not a filing; an 83(b) election counts only with proof of timely submission.

4. Equity and governance

  • Board and stockholder consents or minutes for every material action
  • Officer appointments and delegated signing authority (see Responsibility and Authority Matrix)
  • Issuance approvals plus proof of payment for shares
  • Equity plan approvals, grant agreements and 409A support before options
  • Founder vesting, repurchase and transfer records; 83(b) election filed within 30 days of transfer, with proof
  • Conflict disclosures and related-party approvals
  • Corporate compliance calendar; claims and disputes register

A separate founders’ agreement is not required when ownership, vesting, IP, roles and governance are covered consistently in the definitive documents.
