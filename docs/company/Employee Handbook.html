Employee Handbook

How we work at Semester · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer. Have employment counsel review for each state before issuing.

1. Conduct

  • Treat students, colleagues and customers with respect; no harassment or discrimination of any kind
  • Report concerns to the founder or, if about the founder, to the board or outside counsel; no retaliation
  • Disclose conflicts of interest in writing

2. Information and devices

  • Company devices are managed, encrypted and locked when unattended
  • Never put student data, credentials or customer data in personal accounts, AI tools not on the approved list, or chat outside company systems
  • Approved AI tools only, with no restricted data in prompts unless the tool is under a signed DPA

3. Time, leave and remote work

  • Remote-first; core collaboration hours 10–3 Central
  • Paid holidays, sick leave and parental leave per the offer letter and state law
  • Expenses reimbursed with receipts within 30 days; see Expense Policy

4. Training

Security, FERPA and accessibility training at hire and every year; completion is recorded as evidence.

5. Complaints and discipline

Concerns are investigated promptly and confidentially. Outcomes range from coaching to termination, proportionate and documented.
