U.S.-oriented corporate seed-to-Series-A planning tool · prepared October 6, 2026 · not legal advice, filing software, executed contracts or a readiness certification.

All milestones start Not assessed. Corporate path assumes a corporation; LLCs require a revised checklist. Local browser storage is unencrypted and not shared, audited or access-controlled. Store only non-sensitive metadata, not tax IDs, signatures, credentials, personal student data or confidential agreements. Export a backup; this tool does not submit filings, send reminders or change external systems.

Tracker controls

Candidate filing dates

These dates are simple calendar-day offsets, not counsel-verified deadlines. They do not apply holiday/weekend adjustments or other statutory relief. State notices and recurring obligations need separate assessment. An 83(b) election is conditional and personal to the taxpayer; Form D is offering/exemption-dependent.

Official IRS Form 15620 · SEC filing guidance

Dependency timeline

Gate order: entity → governance/IP → founder issuance → seed → diligence → Series A. Customer readiness is a parallel track. Dependencies are conservative planning gates, not universal statutory order. A prerequisite is resolved only when Verified complete or N/A with justification. Verification requires an evidence reference; N/A requires a reason. This tool cannot authenticate evidence.

Agreement draft builder

Outputs are counsel-review starting drafts. Unknown facts stay bracketed. They are not customized legal advice or signature-ready agreements. SLA values and liability choices are never filled by default. Do not commit to controls or data-use restrictions until product and provider behavior have been verified.

Complete known facts, then preview a draft. Unfilled fields remain placeholders.
