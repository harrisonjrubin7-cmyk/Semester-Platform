Pilot Evidence Plan and Conversion Playbook

What a pilot must prove, and how it turns into a contract · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Before the pilot

  • Signed pilot agreement and Student Data Privacy Addendum
  • Named sponsor, IT contact, registrar contact
  • Agreed success criteria and decision date in writing

2. Success criteria (example, Vanderbilt)

MetricBaselineTarget by week 10
Weekly active students (of invited)—≥ 55%
Students completing an Action Center item per week—≥ 40%
Advisor appointment booking timeCurrent median−30%
Registration-hold resolution before windowCurrent %+15 pts
Student satisfaction (CSAT)—≥ 4.2 / 5
Support tickets per 100 users—≤ 3
Accessibility issues open, severity high—0

3. Cadence

  • Week 0: kickoff and baseline
  • Weeks 2, 5, 8: evidence review with sponsor
  • Week 10: decision meeting with evidence pack
  • Week 12: contract or documented no-go with reasons

4. Conversion

If targets are met, propose a 3-year agreement starting with the modules used in the pilot, pilot fee credited. If not, agree a fix plan with one extension at no cost, or end and delete data per the addendum.
