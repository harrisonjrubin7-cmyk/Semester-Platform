2. Corrections to keep in mind

  • FERPA isn’t satisfied by a statement: the school-official pathway depends on the real service relationship, direct control, legitimate educational interest, authorized use and redisclosure limits
  • A school email or teacher checkbox isn’t enough authorization: school consent under COPPA is limited to the educational context with notice; other uses need parental consent
  • An adult student or professor signing up doesn’t authorize importing institutional records
  • SOC 2, HECVAT and an accessibility report are different things; a template is not a completed assessment
