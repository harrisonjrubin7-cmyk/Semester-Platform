System Integration and Migration Playbook

How Semester connects to SIS and LMS, and moves a domain only when it’s proven safe · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Principle

Institutions adopt Semester alongside Banner, PeopleSoft, Workday Student, Canvas, Brightspace or Blackboard. A domain moves to Semester as the system of record only after testing, reconciliation, operational readiness and formal approval. Until then the existing system stays authoritative.

3. Domain migration ladder

4 · CutoverSemester becomes record of truth for the domainRegistrar/owner sign-off; rollback plan tested
5 · RetireOld feature turned offOne full term with no rollback

4. Rollback

Every cutover has a named owner, a tested rollback to the previous system, and a frozen export taken immediately before. Rollback is a decision, not an incident.
