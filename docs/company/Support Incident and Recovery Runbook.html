Support Incident and Recovery Runbook

Severity, response targets, escalation and restore · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Severity

SevDefinitionFirst responseUpdate cadence
1Outage, data exposure, wrong official record15 min, 24/7Every 30 min
2Major feature down for an institution1 hour, business hoursEvery 2 hours
3Feature degraded, workaround exists1 business dayDaily
4Question or minor defect2 business daysOn resolution

2. Incident steps

  1. Declare; assign incident commander
  2. Contain: kill switches, revoke access, freeze rollout
  3. Assess scope and whether personal data or education records were involved
  4. Notify: institution contacts per contract (education records within 72 hours of confirmation or sooner where law requires)
  5. Recover; verify with the institution
  6. Postmortem within 5 business days; actions tracked

3. Restore

  • Daily backups; point-in-time recovery 7 days
  • Restore tested quarterly into an isolated environment; results recorded as evidence
  • After restore, reapply deletions recorded since the backup
  • RTO 4 hours, RPO 1 hour (targets, pending first test)
