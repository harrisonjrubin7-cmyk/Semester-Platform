Accessibility Remediation Plan

Turning the Accessibility Conformance Report into fixes with dates · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Process

  1. Independent audit against WCAG 2.2 AA before the first institutional contract
  2. Every finding enters the backlog with severity (blocker, high, medium, low) and owner
  3. Blockers fixed before release; high within 30 days; medium within 90
  4. Fix verified with the assistive technology that found it
  5. ACR updated after each audit and every major release
