Advisor Guide

Using Semester with your caseload · Draft · October 2026

Guide draft.

1. What you can see

Only students currently assigned to you, and only what advising needs: audit, plans they share, shared notes, holds status. You never see students’ private notes, drafts or Brain conversations. Access ends the day an assignment ends.

2. Weekly routine

  1. Open Advising home: alerts are ordered by real deadlines (registration window, unresolved hold)
  2. Message or refer; referrals share only what you write, with the office you choose
  3. Review shared what-if plans; recommend exceptions (the registrar approves)
  4. Keep notes: choose “student can see” or “advisors only”

3. What you can’t do

  • Approve substitutions or waivers without a delegated grant
  • Register or drop a student
  • Read records outside your caseload; requests for more access are logged
