Reconciliation and Audit Trail Ledger

How every governed action is recorded and proven · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. What gets recorded

Every registration change, grade posting, record correction, financial transaction, exception decision, permission change and configuration change writes an immutable audit event.

FieldMeaning
event_idUnique, idempotency-keyed
actor and roleWho acted, under which role and relationship
authorityDelegation or approval that permitted it
action and targetWhat changed, on which record
before / afterValue hashes, not raw restricted data
sourceRequest, job or integration that triggered it
timeServer time, UTC
chain hashHash of previous event for tamper evidence

2. Reconciliation

  • Grades: SIS read-back after every posting; mismatch opens a case
  • Money: double-entry ledger balanced per posting; daily processor and bank reconciliation
  • Enrollment: nightly comparison of Semester and SIS rosters
  • Exceptions: decisions must name what they supersede; no “latest wins”

3. Access for auditors

Registrars and auditors get read-only, scoped exports with the chain verified. Retention follows the institution’s schedule; preservation holds override deletion.
