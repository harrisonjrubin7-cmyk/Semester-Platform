Customer Onboarding and Success Plan

From signature to a healthy first term · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Onboarding checklist

  1. Kickoff: sponsor, IT, registrar, advising contacts; success criteria from the pilot plan
  2. Tenant setup and verified administrators
  3. SSO and provisioning tested in sandbox
  4. Integrations: SIS mirror, LMS (LTI) per course
  5. Data mapping workbook signed off
  6. UAT with institution testers; defects triaged
  7. Training: staff sessions + student orientation materials
  8. Launch; hypercare for 2 weeks with daily check-ins
  9. Acceptance certificate; handoff to customer success
