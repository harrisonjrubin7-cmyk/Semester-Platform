Administrator Setup Manual

Setting up an institution tenant · Draft · October 2026

Operator manual draft.

1. Steps

  1. Verify administrators (two named people) and record delegated roles
  2. Connect SSO (SAML or OIDC); test in sandbox; enable SCIM provisioning if available
  3. Load the academic calendar record (terms, sessions, credit unit, load rules, effective dates)
  4. Connect the SIS mirror and approve the field map; freshness shows on every screen
  5. Connect the LMS (LTI 1.3 registration, NRPS, AGS)
  6. Set retention and export settings per your records schedule
  7. Choose enabled modules and AI features per role
  8. Run UAT with your testers; record acceptance
  9. Name joint incident contacts

2. Never automatic

Official writes (grades, registration, records) stay off until a separate, recorded authorization per domain. Building a control is not authorization to use it.
