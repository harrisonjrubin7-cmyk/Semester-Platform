Campus Ambassador Playbook

Recruiting and supporting student and faculty advocates · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Roles

RoleWhoCommitmentSupport
Student ambassador2–4 per campus, varied majors4 hours/week, one termStipend, training, swag, reference letter
Faculty champion1–3 per campusUse in one course; share feedbackEarly features, direct product line
Club partnerStudent organizationsRun events through SemesterEvent credits

2. Activities

  • Orientation tables and QR installs
  • Workshops: “Plan your semester in 20 minutes”
  • Peer help in the first two weeks of term
  • Feedback sessions every 3 weeks

3. Rules

  • Ambassadors are paid and disclose it in any post
  • No access to other students’ data; no sales targets on individuals
  • Never promise features or outcomes
  • Follow the Community Guidelines and the institution’s solicitation policy
