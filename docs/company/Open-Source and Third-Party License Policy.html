Open-Source and Third-Party License Policy

What Semester may use, and how ownership stays clean · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Allowed without review

MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC and similar permissive licenses, with notices kept.

2. Needs review

LGPL, MPL, EPL; any copyleft (GPL, AGPL) is not used in shipped code without founder and counsel approval. Fonts, images, datasets and course content need a recorded license.

3. Process

  • Dependency scanning in CI flags license and vulnerability issues
  • SBOM generated at each release
  • Third-party license register lists every non-code asset with source and license
  • Never paste code from unknown sources or AI output with unclear provenance into the product without review
