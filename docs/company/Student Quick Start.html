Student Quick Start

Your first 15 minutes in Semester · Draft · October 2026

Public guide draft. Check every step against the live product before publishing.

1. Sign in

Use your school sign-in (the same one as your email). If your school isn’t on Semester yet, you can still use the free Studio and Brain with your own account.

2. Your first three things

  1. Today: see what’s due, what’s blocked and where each item comes from
  2. Ask Brain: “Plan my week” gives you three next steps with sources. It never changes your grades or registration
  3. Studio: start a document or notes for a class; your private work stays private

3. Before registration

  • Open Plan registration to see which requirements still need a course
  • Check holds early; each hold shows the office that can clear it
  • A plan or cart is never enrollment. You still submit when your window opens

4. Your privacy

  • Notes, drafts and Brain chats are yours; staff can’t see them
  • Choose what Brain can use under Context
  • Export or delete your data any time in Settings

5. Help

In-app help, or support@semester.app. For accessibility help: accessibility@semester.app.
