Monthly Close Checklist

Closing the books every month · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. By business day 5

  1. Reconcile every bank and card account
  2. Reconcile Stripe payouts to bank deposits and to the ledger
  3. Record invoices issued and payments received; update AR aging
  4. Record bills and accruals; update AP aging
  5. Update deferred revenue schedule for annual institutional contracts (recognized monthly over the term)
  6. Payroll and contractor payments recorded
  7. Review P&L and balance sheet against budget; note variances over 10%
  8. Update cash forecast and runway
  9. Founder sign-off; lock the period

2. Revenue recognition (summary)

Subscriptions recognized ratably over the service period; implementation fees recognized as delivered or over the term if not distinct (confirm with the accountant); pilot fees credited to year one are deferred until the credit is used. A formal memo is prepared before the first annual contract.
