Grant and Institutional Brief

Two-page brief for boards, foundations and grant committees · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Summary

Semester unifies the systems students use to plan, learn, register, get support and prepare for careers. It aims to raise persistence and on-time completion by making next steps clear, especially for first-generation and transfer students who navigate fragmented systems with the least help.

2. Need

  • Students use many separate systems each term
  • Registration holds and missed deadlines are leading, preventable causes of stop-out
  • Advisors spend significant time locating information across systems

3. Approach

  • Action Center with official sources and responsible offices
  • Coordinated advising and referrals
  • Governed AI that explains, never decides
  • Modular adoption alongside existing systems
