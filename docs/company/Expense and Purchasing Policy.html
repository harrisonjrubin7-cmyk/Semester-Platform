Expense and Purchasing Policy

Spending company money · Draft · October 2026

Internal working draft. Figures are planning assumptions to be replaced with actuals; nothing here is a commitment to a customer.

1. Approvals

AmountApproverNeeds
Up to $500SpenderReceipt within 30 days
$500–$5,000FounderReceipt + business purpose
Over $5,000 or any annual contractFounderQuote; vendor intake if data is involved
Any vendor touching student dataFounder + TrustVendor Intake and Due Diligence Checklist

2. Corporate cards

  • Business use only; no personal charges
  • Receipts attached in the expense tool within 7 days
  • Lost card reported same day

3. Bank-detail changes

Any change to a vendor’s or customer’s bank details is confirmed by phone using a number already on file, never one in the request. Suspected fraud follows the incident runbook.
