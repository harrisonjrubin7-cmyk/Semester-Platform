Privacy Rights Request Procedure

Handling access, correction and deletion requests · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Who decides

For institutional accounts the institution is in charge of education records; Semester forwards requests to the institution within 2 business days and helps it respond. For self-serve accounts Semester responds directly.

2. Steps

  1. Receive via privacy@semester.app or in-app; log with date
  2. Verify identity using the account’s sign-in, never by asking for extra sensitive data
  3. Route: institutional → institution contact; self-serve → Semester
  4. Fulfil: export in open formats; correction; deletion across primary stores, search indexes, derived data, subprocessors; backups expire within 90 days
  5. Check for preservation holds before deleting
  6. Respond within 30 days (or the shorter legal deadline); record outcome

3. Education records

Inspection requests under FERPA are the institution’s to answer within 45 days; Semester delivers its records to the institution within 10 business days.
