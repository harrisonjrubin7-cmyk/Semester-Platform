Vendor Data Processing Agreement

Inbound: Semester [legal entity] as controller or processor, and [Vendor] as subprocessor · Effective [date]

Draft for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Brackets [ ] mark details to fill in. Check state law, institutional procurement rules and any counterparty paper before use.

Semester signs this with every vendor that touches personal data, so its commitments to institutions flow down. Where a vendor's own DPA is used, it must meet every point here.

1. Instructions

Vendor processes personal data only on Semester's documented instructions, for [service], and tells Semester if an instruction seems unlawful.

2. Student data

If Vendor processes education records, it is bound by the same FERPA and state-law limits as Semester: no sale, no advertising, no general model training, no re-disclosure.

3. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to named personnel, MFA, logging, and an annual independent assessment (SOC 2 Type II or equivalent) shared on request.

4. Sub-subprocessors

Vendor lists its subprocessors in Exhibit A and gives [30] days' notice of changes. Semester may object; if unresolved, Semester may terminate without penalty.

5. Breach

Vendor notifies Semester within [24] hours of becoming aware of a personal-data breach, with facts, affected data and containment steps, and cooperates fully.

6. Location and transfers

Data stays in [United States] unless Semester agrees in writing; any transfer uses a lawful mechanism.

7. Audit, return and deletion

Semester may audit on reasonable notice or rely on the assessment report. At the end of the service Vendor returns or deletes data within [30] days and certifies it.
