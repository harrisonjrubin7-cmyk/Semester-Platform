AI Addendum

Terms for AI features in institutional deployments · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Scope

Applies to Semester Brain, the Learning Tutor, Instructor Assistant, explanation features and any later AI feature listed in the Order Form.

2. Commitments

  • AI features never issue grades, change academic records, enroll or drop students, approve financial actions, or make discipline, admission, aid or eligibility decisions
  • Customer Data and Education Records are not used to train general models; model providers are under zero-retention terms
  • Only context the user selected or the course policy allows is sent; private notes, other courses and direct messages are off by default
  • Responses cite their sources; uncertainty is stated
  • Instructors can set per-assignment AI rules; Semester enforces them
  • Institution can turn any AI feature off for any role

3. Providers

Model providers are listed in the Subprocessor Register with data handled and region. 30 days’ notice before adding one.

4. Records

Prompts, context IDs, model version and outputs are logged for 1 year for review and audit, then deleted. Students can export or delete their own history.
