Contractor will provide [services] as described in each statement of work. Contractor controls how and when the work is done and supplies its own equipment.

2. Fees and invoicing

$[ ] per [hour / milestone], invoiced [monthly], net [30]. Expenses only with prior written approval. Contractor handles its own taxes and benefits.

Deliverables are works made for hire for Semester; to the extent not, Contractor assigns all rights in them to Semester. Contractor keeps pre-existing tools and grants Semester a perpetual licence to any embedded in deliverables.

Same duties as the Confidentiality and IP Assignment agreement. Contractor gets access to student or customer data only if the statement of work requires it, through Semester's access controls, never by export to personal systems.

5. Classification

Contractor is not an employee. Semester will review the relationship against [state] classification tests (for example the ABC test) before signing and at renewal.
