Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement

Every employee and founder signs on or before day one · Semester [legal entity] · [Name]

Draft for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Brackets [ ] mark details to fill in. Check state law, institutional procurement rules and any counterparty paper before use.

1. Confidentiality

You keep Semester's confidential information secret during and after your work, and use it only for Semester. This includes customer and student data, source code, plans, pricing and security details. Student data has extra rules: access it only within your granted role and scope, for the task at hand.

2. Inventions

You assign to Semester all rights in work you create within the scope of your role or using Semester resources ("Company Inventions"), and will sign what is needed to record that. Work created entirely on your own time, without Semester resources, and unrelated to Semester's business is yours, to the extent required by [state] law (for example Cal. Labor Code §2870). List any prior inventions you are keeping in Exhibit A.

3. Open source

Contribute Semester code to open-source projects only under the open-source policy, and never include confidential information or student data.

4. Returning property

When you leave, return all devices, documents and access, and confirm no Semester data remains on personal devices.

5. Non-solicitation

For [12] months after leaving you won't solicit Semester employees to leave, where enforceable under [state] law. This agreement contains no non-compete.

6. Protected rights

Nothing here stops you reporting possible legal violations to a government agency, discussing wages or working conditions, or making disclosures protected by whistleblower law (including the Defend Trade Secrets Act immunity notice).
