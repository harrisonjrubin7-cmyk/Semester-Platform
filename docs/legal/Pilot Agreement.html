1. Purpose

A bounded, one-term evaluation of the Semester Registration Readiness pilot for an agreed cohort, to decide whether to proceed to an annual subscription. The pilot is not a production commitment by either party.

3. Success measures

The parties agree baseline, target and data source for each measure before launch, signed by Institution's executive sponsor. Semester reports results within 30 days of term end. A measure that cannot be sourced is reported as "not measured", never estimated.

4. Responsibilities

  • Semester: configuration, SSO and SIS read setup, staff training, pilot support per the Support Plan, weekly status, incident notice per the DPA.
  • Institution: executive sponsor and operational champion, IT/SSO contact, registrar data owner, cohort communication, timely access to test accounts.

5. Data

The Data Protection Addendum and Student Data Privacy Addendum apply. Semester acts as a school official with a legitimate educational interest under FERPA, under Institution's direct control. Institution remains the authority for every official record. At pilot end Institution chooses: convert (data retained under the annual agreement) or exit (data returned in a documented export and deleted within [30] days, with a deletion certificate).

6. Fees

[Fixed pilot fee $[ ] / no fee]. If Institution signs an annual Order Form within [60] days of the results report, the pilot fee is credited against year-1 fees.

7. Ending early

Either party may end the pilot on [10] business days' written notice. Semester may suspend immediately to contain a security incident. On ending, Section 5 exit applies; prepaid fees for unused weeks are refunded pro rata.

8. Publicity

No use of Institution's name, marks or results in marketing without its prior written approval of the exact text.

9. General

The Master Services Agreement governs anything this agreement does not address. If they conflict, this agreement governs the pilot only. Liability limits in the MSA apply.
