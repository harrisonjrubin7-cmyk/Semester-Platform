Acceptable Use Policy

Applies to every Semester account · Version [1.0] · Effective [date]

Draft for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Brackets [ ] mark details to fill in. Check state law, institutional procurement rules and any counterparty paper before use.

This policy is part of the Terms of Service and each institution's agreement. It exists to keep students, staff and institutions safe. Breaking it can lead to removed content, a suspended account or a report to the institution.

1. You may not

  • Access another person's account, records or shared items without their permission.
  • Probe, scan or test the service's security except through our published security reporting process.
  • Upload malware, or overload the service on purpose.
  • Use Semester to harass, threaten, or share someone's private information.
  • Use Ask Semester to complete work in a way your course rules don't allow.
  • Scrape the service, or resell access.
  • Pretend to be a staff member, an office or another student.
  • Use the marketplace for prohibited items or off-platform payment scams.

2. Staff and operators

  • Open student records only for the task in front of you, within your granted scope.
  • Never share credentials, or bypass approvals or MFA.
  • Use support access only inside a student-approved, time-limited window.

3. Reporting

Report misuse in the app (Report) or to abuse@[domain]. Report security issues to security@[domain]; we don't pursue good-faith researchers who follow the security policy.

4. Enforcement

We act proportionately and, for students, together with the institution's conduct process. You can appeal once through the in-app appeal.
