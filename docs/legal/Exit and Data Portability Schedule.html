Exit and Data Portability Schedule

What happens to institution data at the end of a contract · Draft · October 2026

Draft for review by a licensed attorney and the institution’s privacy office. Not legal advice. Statuses describe what Semester has, not certifications it doesn’t hold.

1. Export

  • Available any time and for 30 days after termination
  • Formats: CSV and JSON for records, original files for uploads, PDF for generated documents, OneRoster where applicable
  • Includes audit logs for the institution’s tenant

2. Transition assistance

Up to 20 hours of help moving data to a successor system at no charge; more under a SOW.

3. Deletion

  • Active stores and derived data (search, embeddings, caches) within 60 days of export confirmation or day 30, whichever is later
  • Backups expire within 90 days; restored backups reapply deletions first
  • Subprocessors instructed to delete on the same schedule
  • Destruction certificate on request
  • Preservation holds named by the institution override deletion

4. Students

Students keep their own private work (notes, portfolio) and can export it before the institution’s deletion date.
