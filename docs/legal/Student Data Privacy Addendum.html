1. Status under FERPA

Semester is designated a school official with a legitimate educational interest (34 CFR §99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)). It performs an institutional service the Institution would otherwise use employees for, is under the Institution's direct control over use and maintenance of education records, and uses them only for the purposes in the Order Form.

2. What Semester will never do

  • Sell student data, or use it for targeted advertising.
  • Build a profile of a student for any purpose other than the contracted service.
  • Use education records to train general-purpose AI models.
  • Re-disclose education records except to subprocessors listed in the DPA, under equally strict terms, or as required by law with prior notice to the Institution where lawful.
  • Change an official record. The Institution's systems remain authoritative.

3. Students and families

Requests to inspect, amend or delete records go to the Institution, which directs Semester. Semester responds to Institution instructions within [10] business days. Family access exists only by a student's own time-limited grant, or as the Institution directs under FERPA.

4. State law

Where a state student-privacy law applies (for example California SOPIPA, Illinois SOPPA, New York Ed Law §2-d), its stricter terms control. Exhibit A lists the states in scope and any state-specific terms. Semester will sign the Student Data Privacy Consortium National DPA where the Institution prefers it.

5. Security and breach

Security controls are in the Security Addendum. Semester notifies the Institution of a breach of education records without undue delay and within [72] hours of confirmation, with the facts known, records affected and containment steps, and supports the Institution's notices.

6. Return and deletion

On termination, or on request, Semester returns data in a documented export and deletes it, including backups on their normal cycle (no longer than [35] days), and provides a written certificate. Aggregate, de-identified data that cannot reasonably be re-identified may be kept for product improvement only.
