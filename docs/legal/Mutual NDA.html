Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

Between Semester [legal entity] and [Counterparty] · Effective [date]

Draft for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Brackets [ ] mark details to fill in. Check state law, institutional procurement rules and any counterparty paper before use.

1. Purpose

To evaluate a possible business relationship concerning [purpose] (the "Purpose").

2. Confidential Information

Non-public information one party (the discloser) shares with the other (the recipient), marked or reasonably understood as confidential. It excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, already known to the recipient without restriction, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

Student and personal data must not be shared under this NDA. Any such data needs a Data Protection Addendum first.

3. Obligations

  • Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose.
  • Share it only with employees and advisors who need to know and are bound by similar duties.
  • Protect it with at least reasonable care.
  • Disclose it if legally compelled, after prompt notice where lawful.

4. Term

This agreement covers disclosures for [2] years from the effective date; duties last [3] years after each disclosure, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

5. Return

On request, the recipient returns or destroys Confidential Information and confirms in writing. Archival copies kept by automated backup stay confidential.

6. General

No licence or obligation to do business is created. Remedies include injunctive relief. Governed by the laws of [state]. Entire agreement on its subject.
