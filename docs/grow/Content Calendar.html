3. Pillars

  • Trust: privacy, AI without records, accessibility. Every claim cites evidence or says "planned".
  • Workflows: one real job made easier (register, advise, reconcile).
  • Students: practical tips, not hype.
  • Builder: transparent progress from a solo founder.

4. Approval workflow

  1. Draft in the content board with sources.
  2. Self-check: no student data, no uncited numbers, no certification claims we do not hold.
  3. Institution approval if named (allow 5 business days).
  4. Accessibility check: alt text, captions, contrast.
  5. Publish and log the link.
