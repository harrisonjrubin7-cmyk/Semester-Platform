Discovery questions that surface the gap

  • Walk me through what a first-year student does the week registration opens.
  • Where do students go when a hold blocks them, and how do they find who owns it?
  • How do advisors see a student’s plan today, and how does the student control that?
  • What would your security team need to approve AI that touches institutional information?
  • What measure would make a one-term pilot a clear yes for an annual decision?
