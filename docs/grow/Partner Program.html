Partner program

How Semester works with other organizations to reach institutions and deliver value. Draft, October 2026. Partner terms need attorney review before any is signed.

Rule. No partner tier gives access to student records. Data partners act only as subprocessors under the DPA, listed publicly 30 days before use.

1. Why partners

As a solo founder, Semester cannot sell to or implement at many campuses alone. Partners extend reach (introductions), delivery (implementation) and product (integrations) without adding headcount.

4. Joining process

  1. Partner applies through the site contact form.
  2. Fit call (30 min): customers, conflicts, security posture.
  3. Vendor risk review for anyone touching systems (docs/secure/Vendor Risk Policy).
  4. Sign partner terms; add to the partner register.
  5. Onboarding: tutorials, admin manual, sandbox on the test campus.

5. Deal registration

Partners register an opportunity before introducing it. Registration holds for 90 days. First registration wins; conflicts are decided by the founder within 5 business days.

6. Integration partner standards

  • Read-only by default; any write path goes through an audited command.
  • OAuth or signed keys; no shared passwords.
  • Documented rate limits and error behaviour.
  • Accessibility: any partner UI shown inside Semester meets WCAG 2.2 AA.
