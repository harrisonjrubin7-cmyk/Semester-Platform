Business continuity and disaster recovery plan

Owner: founder. Status: draft for review, October 2026. Covers the platform and the company. Tested by drill each quarter.

2. Backups

  • Supabase point-in-time recovery enabled (7+ days); daily logical dump to separate storage, encrypted
  • Storage buckets replicated; evidence bucket versioned
  • Configuration (env, flags, policies) in the repo; secrets in the vault with break-glass copy
  • Restore tested quarterly; time recorded in the evidence register

3. Scenarios and responses

4. Key-person continuity

  • Credentials escrow (sealed, with counsel) for hosting, domain, bank, GitHub org
  • Runbooks current and in the repo
  • Named backup contact for each customer in the order form
  • Monthly check that a second person can sign in to the vault (once hired)
