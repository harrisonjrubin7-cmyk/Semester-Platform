AI governance policy

Owner: founder. Status: draft for review, October 2026. Applies to every AI feature in Semester. Aligned with the NIST AI RMF and its Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1).

Principle: AI can explain an official rule. It cannot become the official rule. Student records never reach an AI provider.

1. Scope

Ask Semester, study cards, writing help, search answers, routing suggestions, moderation pre-screen and any future model use, by any provider.

2. Roles

RoleResponsibility
Founder (AI owner)Approves providers, models and use cases; owns this policy
InstitutionTurns AI on per data type and per course; can switch it off at any time
InstructorSets course AI rules (outline, feedback, practice, write, none)
StudentChooses to use AI; can turn it off; sees sources and limits on every answer
Security leadRuns red-team tests and reviews incidents

3. Allowed and prohibited

AllowedProhibited
Explaining published policies and course material with citationsSending grades, transcripts, holds, aid, accommodation or disciplinary data to a provider
Practice questions from instructor-approved sourcesGenerating graded work where the course rules forbid it
Drafting with the student, labelled AI-assistedMaking or implying an official decision
Suggesting the right office or personClinical, disciplinary, emergency or financial decisions
Pre-screening public community posts for safetyTraining any model on institution or student data

4. Technical controls

  • Single gateway: every request passes scope, policy, budget and content-scan checks before any provider call
  • Content scan blocks student IDs, SSNs, birth dates, GPAs, grades, card numbers and record terms typed into prompts
  • Only approved, institution-published sources are retrievable as context
  • Usage log stores task, outcome and reason codes, never prompts or answers
  • Per-tenant switches and per-course rules read server-side on every call
  • Spend caps per user and tenant; fallback to "ask a person" when a provider fails

5. Provider requirements

  • Written terms: no training on our data, no retention beyond the request, US processing
  • Listed in the subprocessor and AI-provider registries with 30 days’ notice of changes
  • Security review on onboarding and yearly

TestWhenPass

7. Transparency

Every AI answer is labelled AI-assisted and shows sources, the policy that applied, confidence and limits, and a way to ask a person. The Trust Center publishes this policy and the provider registry.

8. Incidents

Any suspected record leakage to a provider is an S1 incident under the incident response plan: switch AI off for the tenant, preserve logs, notify the institution within the DPA deadline.

9. Review

Reviewed every six months, after any AI incident, and before adding a provider or use case.
