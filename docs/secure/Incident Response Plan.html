Incident response plan

Owner: founder (incident commander until a security lead is hired). Status: draft for review, October 2026. Works with docs/operate/Runbooks.html and the DPA.

First move for any suspected data exposure: turn off the affected feature or cohort, preserve evidence, start the incident record.

1. Severity

LevelDefinitionResponse
S1Possible student-data exposure, record sent to AI, cross-tenant access, broad outage15 min, any hour; institution notified within the DPA deadline
S2SSO down, wrong official data shown, critical workflow blocked30 min, 07:00–22:00
S3Feature degraded with workaroundNext business hour
S4Cosmetic or questionNormal queue

3. Steps

  1. Detect (alert, report, support ticket) and open the incident in /ops.
  2. Triage and declare severity.
  3. Contain: kill switch, cohort flag off, revoke credentials, block the path.
  4. Preserve: logs, audit events, request IDs; no deletions.
  5. Assess impact: which records, tenants, people, time window.
  6. Notify: institution security contact (S1 without undue delay, within the DPA deadline); status page for outages; the institution decides student notices.
  7. Eradicate and recover; verify with tests; re-enable only after go/no-go.
  8. Postmortem within 5 business days; corrective actions with owners and dates.

4. Notification template

Use ui_kits/launch-kit/Incident Notice Email.html: what happened, when, who is affected, data that may be affected, what we have done, what you can do now, next update.

5. Exercises

Tabletop each quarter (scenarios: record sent to AI, cross-tenant read, SIS sync showing wrong holds, credential leak). Results feed the runbooks.

6. Evidence

Incident records, postmortems and exercise reports are kept in the evidence register for HECVAT and SOC 2 readiness.
