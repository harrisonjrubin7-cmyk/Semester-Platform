Vendor risk policy

Owner: founder. Status: draft, October 2026. Every vendor has a purpose, a review and an exit. Follows FTC guidance on service-provider oversight.

1. Tiers

TierDefinitionReview
CriticalProcesses student data or runs production (hosting, database, AI provider, email)Security review, DPA, subprocessor notice, yearly re-review
HighCompany data or admin access (GitHub, accounting, payroll)Security questionnaire, MFA enforced, yearly
LowNo sensitive data (design tools, stock assets)Purpose and owner recorded

2. Intake

  1. Purpose and owner
  2. Data classification (T0–T6)
  3. Security and privacy review (SOC 2 report or questionnaire)
  4. Contract terms: confidentiality, breach notice, no training on data, deletion on exit, US processing where required
  5. Access scope: least privilege, MFA, named accounts
  6. Record in /ops vendors and, for critical, the public subprocessor list with 30 days' notice
