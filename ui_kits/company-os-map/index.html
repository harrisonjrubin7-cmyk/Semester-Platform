Prototype built means a working prototype exists — not that it is implemented, tested, deployed or institution-authorized. Production green is tracked per capability in the Green status register.

Two rules that apply to every layer

Students see one task list, one inbox and one identity. They never need to know which office owns a workflow.

AI recommends, summarizes and routes. Final decisions on financial, academic and disciplinary matters stay policy-controlled, and protected records never reach social or open surfaces.

Rollout order
  1. Platform core, Student OS home, communications, Campus Life OS
  2. Admissions OS and student onboarding
  3. Revenue and Finance OS with ledger and reconciliation
  4. Academic and Student Success OS with intervention workflows
  5. Partner and Operations OS, plus advanced Executive OS dashboards

The AI layer and the internal team OS ship alongside each phase rather than as their own phase.
